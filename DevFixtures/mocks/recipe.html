Crispy Lemon Garlic Chicken Thighs

⭐ 4.8 (2,314) · 35 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 lemons, zest and juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Pat chicken dry and season well.
  3. Sear skin-side down 6 minutes until golden.
  4. Add garlic and lemon, roast 25 minutes.
